1. What it is for

It answers: which business lines generate the margin and which ones consume it, which one does the company depend on? It takes the result that Bufins already calculates by line and shows it as an income statement by line, with the revenue mix, the margins and the comparison with last year.

For the manager or owner
  • Which line is the leader and how much it weighs in revenue.
  • Which lines contribute to operating profit and which do not.
  • The AI reading: profitability, concentration and recommended action.
For the accountant or financial analyst
  • The complete income statement of each line, from revenue to net profit.
  • The validation against the Income statement (P&L).
  • Export to Excel, PDF and email.

2. The screen

Choose the company, the scenario and a range of months (with the usual shortcuts: current year, last 6 months, etc.). The figures of the range are added up and compared with the same range of last year. You can also choose the color palette of the report and its exports.

The result is organized in tabs: the key indicators always stay at the top and each tab shows its part — Summary (revenue mix, contribution to profit and margins), Income statement (the matrix by line and the summary), Sales lines (only if the company uses them) and Analysis and comments (AI reading and management comments). When exporting to PDF or image, all the sections are included.

3. Where the figures come from

From the model table ModeloLineasNegocio, the result of the Business Lines model. The report does not run the model: there is data only after it has been run in Model Execution (that is why it carries the “Bufins model information” capsule).

  • The lines are specific to each company. The model stores each account with the name of the line at the end (for example “Ingresos Exp” belongs to the “Exp” line); the report discovers the lines by itself, with no fixed lists.
  • Each line carries the same 16 items: revenue, other and financial income, administration and sales expenses, depreciation and amortization (of cost and of expense), provisions, other expenses, financial expenses, exchange difference, income tax and cost of sales.
  • Only the lines with activity in the chosen range are shown.

Business line and sales line. When the model includes the columns LineaNegocio and LineaVenta, the business line comes from the first and the sales line (the “Type”) from the second. If the business line column is empty (older models) the name at the end of the account is used, as before. The analysis by sales line only appears if the company has data in LineaVenta.

Company with a single business line. Costs and expenses are not distributed between lines: they are taken from the Income statement (P&L) of the whole company, by item, and assigned in full to that single line. Revenue still comes from the lines model, so it matches the sum of the sales lines. The report says so with a footnote.

4. How it is calculated

ItemHow it is obtained
Cost of salesCost of sales (COGS) + depreciation of cost + amortization of cost.
Gross profitRevenue − cost of sales.
Operating expensesAdministration + sales + depreciation and amortization of expense + provisions for receivables and inventories.
Operating incomeGross profit − operating expenses.
EBITDAOperating profit + the four depreciations and amortizations (cost and expense). The model has no EBITDA line.
Non-operating resultOther income + financial income − other expenses − financial expenses − exchange difference.
Net incomeOperating profit + non-operating result − income tax.
MarginsEach profit divided by the revenue of the same line (without revenue there is no margin).
ComparisonThe same range of months of the previous year; if that year has no lines data, no variations are shown.
Sales lines: shareRevenue of the sales line divided by the revenue of all the sales lines in the range.
Sales lines: contribution to the changeVariation in pesos of the line against the previous year divided by the total revenue variation: how much of the growth (or the drop) that line explains.
Sales lines: concentrationSum of the share of the three largest sales lines (shown only with more than three lines).
Sales lines: best monthThe month of the range with the highest revenue of that line; “Months with sales” counts the months with non-zero revenue.

Costs and expenses come from the model as positive values and the report subtracts them; they are not interpreted as negative signs.

5. Match with the Income statement

A green strip indicates that, for the same range, the revenue and net profit of all the lines added together match the Income statement (P&L) within a tolerance of one peso. If they do not match, the strip turns red and shows the difference: it is a signal to review how costs were assigned between lines or whether the Business Lines model and the P&L model were run with the same data.

6. AI reading

  • The Generate button produces three lines: Profitability (which line is the most and least profitable), Concentration (how much the business depends on a single line) and Recommended action. “Ask this report” answers free-form questions about these figures.
  • What is sent to the AI: only the already-calculated summary by line and the total; no detailed accounting data and nothing from other tables is sent.
  • It counts against the company’s AI budget and respects per-user access; if AI is turned off for the company, the block does not appear.

7. Export

Export button, the same as in the other reports, with the company logo and the chosen palette:

  • Excel: sheets “Summary by line”, “Statement by line” and “Monthly mix”; if there are sales lines, also “Sales lines” and “Sales lines mix”.
  • Landscape PDF: summary with indicators, AI reading and comments; mix and margins; the income statement by line; and, if there are sales lines, a page with the share pie chart and the table.
  • Copy as image and Send by email (with the Excel and PDF attached; the sending is recorded in the audit log).

8. Frequently asked questions

I do not see any lines, what do I do?

Run the company’s Business Lines model in Model Execution and choose a range with data. If the company does not use lines, the report has nothing to show.

Why does a line not appear?

Because it had no activity (everything at zero) in the chosen range.

Why do I not see costs or margins by sales line?

Because the model only brings revenue by sales line. Costs, expenses and margins are analyzed by business line.

My company has a single business line, where do its costs and expenses come from?

From the Income statement (P&L) of the whole company: being a single line, all the costs and expenses belong to it and are not broken down between lines.

Why does the analysis by sales line not appear?

Because the company has no sales line data in the Business Lines model, or the model was run before that column existed: run it again in Model Execution.

Why is the match strip red?

The totals of the lines do not match the P&L. Check that both models were run for the same scenario and the same data load.

Why is a margin empty?

Because the line had no revenue in the range: without revenue there is no margin.

Can I compare against the budget?

Not today: the lines model does not carry a budget. The comparison is against the same range of last year.

Who can write comments?

All users with access to the company; only the author or an administrator can edit or delete a comment.
