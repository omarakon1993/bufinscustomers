1. What it is for

It builds the month-end close as a document to present, decide on and sign in a committee: how the month closed, how much money is left, how the year is going month by month, which indicators need attention, and a space to write down the decisions and the signatures.

For the committee or management
  • A single reading page with what matters from the close, without opening several reports.
  • Results against budget, available and projected cash, and the year P&L on one sheet.
  • It can be printed or sent by email, with a decisions box and signatures.
For the finance team
  • It calculates no new figures: it reuses those of the Monthly management report, the P&L and the Cash flow.
  • Each part is a section that can be collapsed; what stays expanded is what gets exported.
  • The close management comments are included and can be written from here.

4. Where the figures come from and how to read them

Everything comes from the model materialized tables (Modelo*); this report does not run the models or calculate figures of its own.

FigureHow it is obtained
Cash at the start of the yearThe “Cash and banks” balance sheet line at the prior year-end. If that balance sheet does not exist, cash cannot be computed and only the flow is shown.
Cash available at the cutoffThe “Cash and banks” of the cutoff month balance sheet; if it does not exist, the calculated cash (opening cash plus actual net flow). If the cutoff is after the last executed month, that last month is used.
Projected cash at DecemberThe cutoff cash plus the projected net flow of the following months, per the model.
AV % of the P&LShare of each line over year-to-date revenue. For costs and expenses, going down against the budget or the prior year is favorable.
EBITDAIt is not a model line: operating profit plus depreciation and amortization.

5. Export

Export button, the same as in the other reports, with the company logo and the chosen palette:

  • Excel: only the expanded sections: “Summary” sheet (results, balance sheet and cash of the month, indicators, lines, decisions and signatures), “P&L”, “Available cash” and “Comments”.
  • Landscape PDF: several landscape pages: cover with the summary, balance sheet and cash; the P&L on its own page; and at the end the indicators, the lines, the comments, the decisions box and the signatures, to print and sign.
  • Copy as image and Send by email (with the Excel and PDF attached; the sending is recorded in the audit log).

6. Frequently asked questions

The month I am looking for does not appear

The list only brings months with revenue in the income statement. Run the company models and choose the company again.

A section does not appear

That part of the model (balance sheet, flow, indicators or lines) has no information for the month. The other sections are not affected.

How is it different from the Monthly management report?

It uses the same figures, but lays them out as a committee document: it adds available and projected cash, the one-page P&L and the decisions and signatures spaces. They are different menu options, with different permissions.

Does it have an AI reading?

No. For an AI reading use the Monthly management report or each individual report.

Can I choose several months?

No: it is the close of one month. For a range use the individual reports.
