1. What it is for

It answers: what does the company own and owe at a given date, is its financial structure healthy? It shows the balance sheet (assets, liabilities and equity) at the cut-off month you choose, compared with another reference date.

The balance sheet is a snapshot, not a flow. Each value is the balance at the cut-off month; months are never added up. The rest of the range only feeds the trends.

For the manager or owner
  • How much the company has in assets, how much it owes and how much is equity.
  • Whether liquidity and leverage improve or worsen against last year’s close.
For the accountant or financial analyst
  • Each account with its balance, its weight over total assets and its variance.
  • The check that Assets = Liabilities + Equity.

2. The screen

Choose the company, the scenario and the range of months; the cut-off month is the right end of the range. Then press Generate.

  • Compare against: the previous year-end (December 31; the default, the accounting standard), the previous month, the same month of the previous year, the budget or the projection for the same cut-off month.
  • Group by (trends only) and Unit (pesos, thousands or millions).
  • Report styles: lets you choose the color palette of the report (Bufins by default; also the company's corporate colors or a custom palette). The choice is applied when you press Generate and reaches every output (screen, PDF, image, Excel and email). The company logo is shown next to its name and does not depend on the palette.

3. Where the figures come from

From the model table ModeloBalance (and, to compare against the budget or the projection, from ModeloBalancePpto). The report does not run the model: there is data only after it has been run in Data → Model execution.

  • Each line is matched with its classification (REL_Balance). The totals (current assets, liabilities, equity…) are the ones the model computes.
  • When comparing against the budget or the projection, lines are matched by their description (not by their order number, which changes from one month to another).
  • If the comparison has no data, the balance is shown without variance (“—”).

4. How it is calculated

ItemHow it is obtained
Vertical %Line balance ÷ total assets at the same cut-off.
Working capitalCurrent assets − current liabilities.
Current ratioCurrent assets ÷ current liabilities, shown as “1.8x”. “Not applicable” if current liabilities are zero.
Total leverageTotal liabilities ÷ total assets. “Not applicable” if assets are zero.
Balance checkAssets − (Liabilities + Equity). A difference of up to 10 pesos is tolerated for model rounding.
Variance colorsIn assets and equity, going up is favorable; in liabilities, going up is unfavorable. The leverage indicator follows the same logic.
Grouped trendsWith quarter or year, each point is the snapshot of the group’s last month with data (not a sum).

There is an optional second check: the year-to-date net profit of the income statement against the “profit for the year” line of the balance sheet. If the balance sheet has no such line, the check is skipped without affecting anything else.

5. AI reading

  • The Generate button produces three lines: Liquidity, Solvency and Working capital.
  • What is sent to the AI: only the already-calculated summary of the balance sheet at cut-off, its comparisons and the indicators; no detailed accounting data and nothing from other tables is sent.
  • It counts against the company’s AI budget and respects per-user access; if AI is turned off for the company, the block does not appear.
  • The reading is a support tool: always check it against the figures on screen.

6. Export

Export button, the same as in the other reports, with the company logo and the chosen palette:

  • Excel: the balance sheet as shown on screen, with the company logo.
  • Landscape PDF: summary with the indicators and the AI reading, the structure and the balance sheet table; footer with company, cut-off and the confidentiality notice.
  • Copy as image and Send by email (with the Excel and PDF attached; the sending is recorded in the audit log).

7. Frequently asked questions

Why are there no “Period” and “Year-to-date” as in the income statement?

Because the balance sheet is a snapshot at cut-off: adding balances of several months would make no sense.

It says “Mismatch of $X”, what does it mean?

That Assets are not equal to Liabilities + Equity within the 10-peso tolerance. Check that the model was run completely for that scenario and data load.

Why do the current ratio or leverage say “Not applicable”?

Because the denominator is zero (current liabilities or total assets).

What is it compared with by default?

With the previous year-end (December 31), the usual reference in accounting standards. You can change it in “Compare against”.

Why is a liability variance red when it went up?

Because more liabilities is unfavorable; in assets and equity, going up is shown as favorable.

Can I compare against the budget?

Yes: “Compare against → Budget” or “Projection” uses the budgeted or projected balance sheet for the same cut-off month.
