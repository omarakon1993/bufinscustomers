Dynamic timeline

How to pick any variable from the models, see its month-by-month evolution, compare it across scenarios and export the result.

1. What it is for

It answers: how has this figure evolved month by month, how does it compare with another scenario? It is the most flexible report: you pick any variable from the model result tables and see its evolution over time, without building anything by hand.

For the manager or owner
  • Follow a specific figure (sales, profit, cash…) and see whether it is improving.
  • Put up to eight variables on the same chart to compare them.
For the accountant or financial analyst
  • Compare Scenario 1 against Scenario 2 for the same variable.
  • Export the full series to Excel with an editable chart.

2. The screen

The filters are in two rows: What to see and Range and grouping. Then press Generate report; nothing is queried until then.

Description / Indicator(s)Opens a selector with search. You can choose up to 8 variables and save combinations you use often.

3. Where the figures come from

From the Model* tables filled by the model run (results, balance sheet, budgeted balance sheet, cash flow, statement of cash flows, business lines and treasury). It is the only source: the report never reads the original uploaded data.

  • Only the tables, variables and months that already have data for the chosen company and scenario appear.
  • If the table has no data, the report says so (“This table has no data loaded in the model for this company and scenario yet”).

4. How it is calculated

ItemHow it is obtained
Value of a periodWith “Month”, the month’s value. With “Quarter” or “Year”, the sum of the group’s months.
Variation of the last periodLast value against the previous period’s value, in percent.
Average of the rangeThe average of the values of all the periods included.
ComparisonThe same variable from the other scenario, period by period, and its difference from the main series.

A variable that is a balance (like those of the balance sheet) makes no sense when added across months: for those figures it is better to group by “Month” or look at a single cut-off.

5. Export

Export button, the same as in the other reports, with the company logo and the chosen palette:

  • Excel: the series being viewed, with an editable native chart (if a value changes, the chart updates) and the same options as on screen (chart type, unit, hidden series).
  • Landscape PDF: you are asked what to include: chart only (with the key indicators) or chart with data (plus the full table of the series).
  • Copy as image and Send by email (with the Excel and PDF attached; the sending is recorded in the audit log).

6. Frequently asked questions

It will not let me compare with another scenario

The comparison is available only when a single variable is chosen, and the other scenario must have data.

Why doesn’t the variable I am looking for appear?

Because that table has no data for the chosen company and scenario, or the model has not been run. Run it in Data → Model execution.

How many variables can I plot at once?

Up to eight on the same chart. Combinations you use often can be saved.

What happens if I group by quarter or year?

Each point is the sum of its months. Figures that are balances are best viewed by month.

How do I switch between lines and bars?

With the chart selector (Lines / Bars); there is also a switch between Chart and Table.

What does the PDF include?

When exporting you choose between “Chart only” and “Chart with data”.
