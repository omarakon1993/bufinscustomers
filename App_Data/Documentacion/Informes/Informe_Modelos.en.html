Models report

How to query, filter and export the tables produced by running the models.

1. What it is for

It answers: what data did the model run produce? It is the direct query of the result tables (income statement, balance sheet, cash flows, business lines, treasury…): pick the company and the table, filter and download.

For the manager or owner
  • See the exact numbers that feed the other reports.
  • Quickly check that a model has already been run and what it contains.
For the accountant or financial analyst
  • Filter by any field and export the result to Excel.
  • Query several tables at once.

2. The screen

Choose the company, the scenario, the year (and, depending on the table, the month) and one or more tables. The Query button changes its text according to your choice (“Query 3 selected tables”, “Query all tables”).

  • Several tables: they are queried one after another and each one appears in its own tab.
  • Filter results: on top of what was queried you can add field filters (equals, contains, starts or ends with, greater or less than…) shown as chips that can be removed one by one.
  • Numbers are shown with thousands and decimal separators; numeric filters compare the real value, not the formatted text.
  • Report styles: lets you choose the color palette of the report (Bufins by default; also the company's corporate colors or a custom palette). The choice is applied when you press Generate and reaches every output (screen, PDF, image, Excel and email). The company logo is shown next to its name and does not depend on the palette.

Bufins model information. This data only exists after the models have been run in Data → Model execution.

3. Where the figures come from

Only from the Model* tables (results, balance sheet, budgeted balance sheet, balance sheet difference, cash flow, statement of cash flows, business lines and treasury). This is a platform rule: the information queried here, and the one used by AI, comes only from the models, never from the original uploaded data.

  • A table without rows for the chosen company and filters shows “No data”.
  • Users can only query their own company or those of their business group.

4. Export

Export button, the same as in the other reports, with the company logo and the chosen palette:

  • Excel: the queried table, with the filters applied.
  • Send by email: to you and/or other people (up to 10), with the Excel attached; the sending is recorded in the audit log.

This report is a data grid, so it has no PDF or image output.

5. Frequently asked questions

It says “No data”, what do I do?

Run the corresponding model in Data → Model execution for the chosen company and scenario, or widen the filters.

Can I query several tables at once?

Yes: choose several in the selector or “All tables”.

Why can’t I see another client’s company?

Because each user only has access to their own company and those of their business group.

Does the Excel include the filters I applied?

Yes, it exports the queried table with its filters.

What is the difference with the Timeline?

The Models report shows the rows as they are; the Timeline plots a variable over the months and lets you compare it across scenarios.
