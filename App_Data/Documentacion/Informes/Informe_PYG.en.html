Income statement (P&L)

What it is, where the figures come from, how margins and EBITDA are calculated, how it compares with the budget and how to read the waterfall.

1. What it is for

It answers: how is the business doing against the budget and against last year, where does the margin come from and where does it go? It shows the company’s income statement — from revenue to net profit — by month, quarter or year, with the comparison you choose.

For the manager or owner
  • How revenue, EBITDA and net profit compare with the budget.
  • A waterfall that explains where each peso of revenue goes until it becomes net profit.
  • A three-line reading (achievement, alert, efficiency) to understand the month in seconds.
For the accountant or financial analyst
  • The full detail by account, with variances in pesos and in percent.
  • Period and year-to-date side by side.
  • Export to Excel and PDF with the same figures as the screen.

2. The screen

Choose the company, the scenario and a range of months (with the usual shortcuts: current year, last 6 months, previous month, etc.). Then press Generate; nothing is queried until then.

  • Group by: month, quarter or year. It only affects the trends; the table always shows the chosen period and the year-to-date.
  • Compare against: the budget (default), the adjusted budget or the forecast. The variance against the same period of the previous year (YoY) is always shown as well.
  • Level of detail: all accounts or subtotals only.
  • Unit: pesos, thousands or millions.
  • Compare with another scenario (optional): adds the figures of a second scenario.
  • Report styles: lets you choose the color palette of the report (Bufins by default; also the company's corporate colors or a custom palette). The choice is applied when you press Generate and reaches every output (screen, PDF, image, Excel and email). The company logo is shown next to its name and does not depend on the palette.

3. Where the figures come from

From the model table ModeloPYG, which is filled when the results model is run in Data → Model execution. The report does not run the model: there is data only after it has been run.

  • Every line of the income statement is matched with its classification (Rel_PYG): the accounts that add up and the subtotals the model already computes (gross profit, EBITDA, net profit…). The report does not invent subtotals: it uses the model’s, so they always match it.
  • The comparison columns come from the same model: budget, adjusted budget and forecast, in addition to the actual value.
  • Each scenario is a parallel copy of the data; the report reads the one you choose.

4. How it is calculated

ItemHow it is obtained
PeriodThe sum of the months in the chosen range.
Year-to-dateThe sum from January to the last month of the range, within the same year.
Var. $Actual − comparison (budget, adjusted budget or forecast).
Var. %Var. $ ÷ comparison. If the comparison is zero there is no percentage.
Margin %Each line divided by the revenue of the same period.
YoY var.Current value against the same period of the previous year. If that year has no loaded data, the column stays empty and a note says so.
WaterfallFive bars: revenue → cost of sales → operating expenses → other items and taxes → net profit. Each step is the difference between two model subtotals, so it always adds up to net profit.
Grouped trendsWith quarter or year, each point is the sum of its months.

5. AI reading

  • The Generate button produces three lines: Achievement (the best of the period), Alert (what moves furthest from the budget) and Efficiency (what happens with costs and margins).
  • What is sent to the AI: only the already-calculated summary of the period, its comparisons and the margins; no detailed accounting data and nothing from other tables is sent.
  • It counts against the company’s AI budget and respects per-user access; if AI is turned off for the company, the block does not appear.
  • The reading is a support tool: always check it against the figures on screen.

6. Export

Export button, the same as in the other reports, with the company logo and the chosen palette:

  • Excel: a sheet with the income statement as shown on screen (percent columns formatted as percentages) and the company logo.
  • Landscape PDF: executive summary, waterfall and trends, and the table with the same level of detail as the screen; footer with company, period and the confidentiality notice.
  • Copy as image and Send by email (with the Excel and PDF attached; the sending is recorded in the audit log).

7. Frequently asked questions

It says there is no data, what do I do?

Run the company’s results model in Data → Model execution and generate the report again. Also check that the scenario and the range have data.

Why is the YoY column empty?

Because the previous year has no loaded data for that company and scenario. The report shows a note.

Why is there no variance percentage on a line?

Because the comparison for that line is zero: dividing by zero makes no sense.

What is the difference between Period and Year-to-date?

Period is the chosen range; Year-to-date runs from January to the last month of the range, in the same year.

Can I compare against another scenario?

Yes, in “Compare with another scenario”. That option is enabled only if the other scenario has data.

Who can write comments?

Every user with access to the company; only the author or an administrator can edit or delete a comment.
